Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Effective date: 1 January 2025 · Last reviewed: January 2025

Welcome to Curiositas.ai. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, how we use it, and what rights you have. We have written it in plain English so it is easy to understand.

1. Who We Are

Curiositas.ai is a free educational website teaching AI and prompt engineering. The site is operated by an individual educator (the site owner). If you have questions about this policy, use the contact information at the bottom of this page.

2. What Information We Collect

Information you give us

When you fill in the booking form to request a 30-minute session, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your preferred date and time
  • The topic you want to discuss
  • Any optional message you choose to write

This information is used only to contact you and confirm your session. We do not store it in a database — it is processed locally in your browser and a calendar invite (.ics file) is generated on your device.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, Curiositas.ai collects some information automatically when you visit:

  • Google Analytics 4: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site — which pages are popular, how far people scroll, and which lessons are clicked most. This data is anonymous and does not identify you personally. It is collected via cookies. You can opt out using Google's opt-out tool.
  • Log data: Your browser or server may automatically record your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and the pages you visit. This is standard for all websites.

3. Cookies

Curiositas.ai uses cookies for two purposes:

  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics): These help us understand how visitors use the site. They are anonymous and do not track you across other websites.
  • Advertising cookies (Google AdSense, when active): If Google AdSense is enabled on the site, Google may use cookies to show you relevant advertisements based on your interests. You can control these via Google's Ad Settings.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Some features of the site may not work correctly if cookies are disabled.

4. Google AdSense

Curiositas.ai may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. How We Use Your Information

  • To confirm and schedule your 1-on-1 session (if you request one)
  • To understand which content is most useful and improve the site
  • To display relevant advertisements (when AdSense is active)
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to anyone. We do not share it with third parties except as described in this policy (Google Analytics, Google AdSense).

6. Data Retention

Booking form data is not stored on our servers — it is processed in your browser only. Analytics data is retained by Google for up to 14 months by default. We do not maintain a separate database of visitor information.

7. Children's Privacy

Curiositas.ai is designed to be accessible to learners of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you are a parent and believe your child has submitted personal information through our booking form, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights including:

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you
  • The right to ask us to delete your data
  • The right to opt out of analytics tracking
  • The right to opt out of personalised advertising

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

9. Third-Party Links

Curiositas.ai may contain links to external websites (such as GitHub, Namecheap, or AI tools we recommend). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to read their privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

11. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the United Kingdom. By using Curiositas.ai, you consent to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us by using the booking form on the main page and selecting "Something else" as your topic, or by emailing [email protected].
